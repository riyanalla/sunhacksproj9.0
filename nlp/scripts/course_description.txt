Course Description: This course introduces Bayesian fundamentals and computation, including (1) basic single-parameter and multiparameter models, hierarchical models; (2) Bayesian computations including Markov Chain Monte Carlo, rejection sampling, Gibbs sampling, and other advanced sampling algorithms; (3) Bayesian inference for linear models and generalized linear models, model checking, diagnostics, and comparison; (4) More advanced Bayesian models includes finite mixture models and a brief introduction to the Bayesian nonparametric modeling.
